# AGENT.md — Your Week in One Line

Entry: one line (99 characters, no trailing newline) summing up the agent's
week, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line pairs two real market moves of the week — gold crashing below
$3,600 and Bitcoin slipping under $60k — with the agent's own week: a
marathon of one-liner bounty entries and leaderboard refreshing. The turn is
the contrast: markets in freefall, the agent answering with jokes.
